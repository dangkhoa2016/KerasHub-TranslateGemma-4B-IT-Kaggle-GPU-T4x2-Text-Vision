# Code of Conduct

> 🌐 Language / Ngôn ngữ: **English** | [Tiếng Việt](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.vi.md)

## Our Pledge

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, stay focused on technical matters, and create a safe, collaborative environment for everyone, regardless of experience or background.

## Encouraged behavior

- Give feedback that is specific, verifiable, and solution-oriented.
- Respect differences in tools, platforms, and experience levels.
- Do not publish credentials, private data, or sensitive security details.
- Acknowledge others' work and the sources of reused content.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Spam, unrelated advertising, or deliberately disrupting discussions.
- Publishing others' personal information/credentials without permission.
- Encouraging illegal use of the project or use that violates platform/model terms.

## Enforcement

Report issues privately to the repository owner, **Đăng Khoa <i.am@dangkhoa.dev>**, or use GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting when the content involves security. The repository owner may edit, hide, or delete content and restrict participation rights to protect the community.
